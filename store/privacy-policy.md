# Vanish Cam: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Vanish Cam processes your camera and microphone only on your own device, inside your browser.

- **What it accesses:** the camera and microphone streams that a web page requests while the extension is active, to cut you out of the video, detect your gestures and snaps, and mix in the sound effect.
- **What it collects:** nothing. Video, audio, images and hand or face positions are never saved, sent, shared or sold. There are no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.
- **What it stores:** your settings and the sound files you add, in the browser's extension storage on your device. Removing the extension deletes them.
- **Third parties:** none. All code and machine-learning models are bundled in the extension and run locally.
